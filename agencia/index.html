How I work

With you from the first visit to the day you get the keys

Homes before they hit the market

Many homes sell without being advertised. I show you the private and upcoming ones first.

A thorough check, especially on the water

Seawall, flood zone, elevation certificate, insurance and permits: I check them with you before you make an offer.

I negotiate with data

Offers based on real sales nearby, not on the asking price.

A closing without the hassle

I coordinate the title company, lender, inspectors and your attorney until you get the keys.

A price buyers will pay

A written pricing study with closed sales and current competition, reviewed every two weeks.

Professional photos, video and floor plans

Photography, drone video and floor plans included, plus staging when it pays off.

Quiet or public sale, your call

Sell quietly to verified buyers, or go to market with a full campaign.

Buyers from everywhere

Listings in English and Spanish that reach buyers across the U.S., Latin America and Europe.

Buying through a company

Many foreign buyers purchase through an LLC. I work with your attorney and accountant from the first offer.

A mortgage without U.S. credit history

I introduce you to lenders with mortgages for foreign nationals.

FIRPTA, no surprises

When a foreign owner sells, part of the price is withheld (FIRPTA). I plan for it with your tax advisor from the day you buy.

Remote closing and home care

Sign by power of attorney or at a consulate, with trusted property managers for your home.

About me

I’m Ingrid Wendy Cabeza, though many people know me as Wendy

I came to the United States as an immigrant, full of dreams and hoping to build a new beginning.

Together with my husband we raised a beautiful family of three children and started developing new homes in Southwest Florida. As a mother and entrepreneur, I know a house means much more than walls and a roof: it is where we watch our children grow, share moments and build memories.

That path inspired me to get my real estate license to help other people reach their own dreams. Today I want to be at your side with the closeness, dedication and clarity I would want when making such an important decision for my family.

Why trust me?

My experience developing new homes with my husband gives me a practical, valuable perspective on finding your home. My commitment is to listen to you, help you explore your options and stay with you so you can make informed decisions with confidence.
