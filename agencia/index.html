Get to know the neighborhoods

Each area has its own feel, schools and prices. Here is what to know about each one.

Miami Beach & the Islands

Star, Sunset and the Venetian Islands: gated causeways, deep-water docks and some of the city’s most private estates.

Key Biscayne

An island village with its own schools, two state and county parks and a slower pace, ten minutes from Brickell.

Coral Gables

Mediterranean Revival streets, strict design review and gated waterfront enclaves along Old Cutler Road.

Coconut Grove

Miami’s oldest neighborhood: a canopy of banyans, sailing clubs on the bay and a walkable village center.

Golden Beach & Sunny Isles

Oceanfront homes with private beach access in Golden Beach, and full-service towers just south in Sunny Isles.

How we work

With you from the first visit to the day you get the keys

Homes before they hit the market

Many homes sell without being advertised. We show you the private and upcoming ones first.

A thorough check, especially on the water

Seawall, flood zone, elevation certificate, insurance and permits: we check them with you before you make an offer.

We negotiate with data

Offers based on real sales nearby, not on the asking price.

A closing without the hassle

We coordinate the title company, lender, inspectors and your attorney until you get the keys.

A price buyers will pay

A written pricing study with closed sales and current competition, reviewed every two weeks.

Professional photos, video and floor plans

Photography, drone video and floor plans included, plus staging when it pays off.

Quiet or public sale, your call

Sell quietly to verified buyers, or go to market with a full campaign.

Buyers from everywhere

Listings in English and Spanish that reach buyers across the U.S., Latin America and Europe.

Buying through a company

Many foreign buyers purchase through an LLC. We work with your attorney and accountant from the first offer.

A mortgage without U.S. credit history

We introduce you to lenders with mortgages for foreign nationals.

FIRPTA, no surprises

When a foreign owner sells, part of the price is withheld (FIRPTA). We plan for it with your tax advisor from the day you buy.

Remote closing and home care

Sign by power of attorney or at a consulate, with trusted property managers for your home.
