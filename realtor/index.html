Where I work

A guide to Miami’s waterfront neighborhoods

Each area has its own rhythm, schools, flood profile and resale market. These are the ones I know street by street.

Miami Beach & the Islands

Star, Sunset and the Venetian Islands: gated causeways, deep-water docks and some of the city’s most private estates.

Key Biscayne

An island village with its own schools, two state and county parks and a slower pace, ten minutes from Brickell.

Coral Gables

Mediterranean Revival streets, strict design review and gated waterfront enclaves along Old Cutler Road.

Coconut Grove

Miami’s oldest neighborhood: a canopy of banyans, sailing clubs on the bay and a walkable village center.

Golden Beach & Sunny Isles

Oceanfront homes with private beach access in Golden Beach, and full-service towers just south in Sunny Isles.

Brickell

The financial district: branded residences, bay views and a short walk to the office, for buyers who want a pied-à-terre.

How I work

One advisor from first showing to closing day

Homes before they are listed

Many of Miami’s best homes trade quietly. You see private and pre-market listings first, with seller disclosures already in hand.

Due diligence for the water

Seawall condition, flood zone and elevation certificate, wind mitigation report, insurance quotes and permit history, all before you make an offer.

Negotiation on the numbers

Offers built from closed sales, price per square foot on the water and days on market, not from the asking price.

A closing you don’t have to manage

I coordinate the title company, lender, inspectors and your attorney through the final walkthrough.

A price the market will pay

A written pricing study of closed sales, active competition and waterfront premiums, updated every two weeks while you are listed.

Presentation

Architectural photography, twilight and drone shoots, film and floor plans. Staging when it pays for itself.

Private or public, your call

Sell quietly to qualified buyers with proof of funds, or launch on the market with a full campaign.

Buyers from everywhere

Marketing in English and Spanish that reaches buyers across the U.S., Latin America and Europe, plus the agents and advisors who represent them.

Buying through a company

Most international clients buy through an LLC or foreign entity. I work alongside your attorney and CPA from the first offer.

Financing without a U.S. credit history

Introductions to private banks and lenders with foreign-national mortgage programs.

Planning for FIRPTA

Foreign sellers usually face FIRPTA withholding at closing. We plan for it with your tax advisor before you buy, not when you sell.

Remote closings & care

Signings by power of attorney or at a consulate, plus property managers who know each city’s rental rules.
