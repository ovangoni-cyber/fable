Investment thesis

Why Miami luxury residential

We invest in one product in one market: single-family homes of $5M and up in Miami-Dade’s established waterfront and school-district neighborhoods.

Tax

No state income tax

Florida has no personal income tax, which keeps drawing high earners, founders and family offices from high-tax states.

Land

Waterfront is finite

The islands and the bay front are built out. New supply comes almost entirely from rebuilding existing lots.

Demand

Buyers from everywhere

Miami is the gateway for buyers from Latin America, Europe and the U.S. Northeast, many of whom pay cash.

Product

Current code sells

Buyers and insurers favor homes built to today’s hurricane and flood codes over older houses on the same street.

Risk

What can go wrong, and how we plan for it

Risk How we manage it
Construction costs riseFixed-price contract with the general contractor, a contingency line in every budget and monthly reviews by the lender’s inspector.
The market softens at saleExit prices underwritten below recent comparable sales, leverage capped at 60% of cost, and the option to hold or lease the home.
A hurricane during constructionBuilder’s risk insurance, High-Velocity Hurricane Zone design and a written storm protocol for every site.
Interest rates riseAn interest reserve funded at closing, and rate caps on floating-rate loans where available.
Permits take longerZoning and permit feasibility reviewed before closing on the land, with schedule buffers in the pro forma.
Your capital is illiquidThere are no redemptions. Invest only money you can leave in place until the home sells.

Questions

Before you invest

Who can invest?

Accredited investors only. For individuals that generally means income above $200,000 in each of the last two years ($300,000 with a spouse or partner) with the same expected this year, or net worth above $1 million excluding your primary residence. Holders of Series 7, 65 or 82 licenses also qualify.

What is the minimum investment?

$250,000 per offering. Some investors spread capital across several projects over time.

Can I invest from outside the United States?

In most cases, yes, often through a company, subject to identity and anti-money-laundering checks. Tax treatment depends on your country of residence, so plan the structure with your own tax advisor.

How are distributions taxed?

Each project LLC is typically taxed as a partnership, and you receive a Schedule K-1 every year. We do not give tax advice; your advisor should review the offering documents.

Can I get my money out before the sale?

No. Interests are illiquid and cannot be redeemed. Capital is returned when the home sells or the project is refinanced.

How much does the sponsor earn?

An acquisition and development fee disclosed in each offering, plus 30% of profits above the 8% preferred return. The sponsor co-invests at least 10% of the equity on the same terms as investors.
